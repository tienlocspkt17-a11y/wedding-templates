# 10 concept DNA — opening choreography

Every invitation in `templates/` is standalone HTML/CSS/vanilla JS. The same 17-module schema is retained, while the opening stage and body layout are intentionally different. The current rework contains no shared two-panel opening veil.

| # | Concept | Visual stage | Guest gesture | 3-second choreography | Body DNA |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | The Morning Vow | silk canopy, warm morning light | pull the gold cord | cord releases → fabric canopy lifts upward → sunlight sweeps across names | centered ceremony, red-ink event panel, framed gallery |
| 02 | Herbarium of Us | pressed leaves under glass | touch the dew drop | dew rises → specimen glass fractures into six shards → names settle | paper specimen sheets, tilted gallery, botanical borders |
| 03 | Geometric Camera | one brass viewfinder and a controlled lens | press “Bấm để khóa nét” | grid breathes → frame corrects its angle → lens exposes the image | names-first editorial hero, dark teal/brass system, single-column events |
| 04 | Quiet Crossing | shoji, red moon, koi shadow | slide the wooden grip | red-moon iris contracts → ripples expand → koi shadow passes | soft organic section shapes, washi palette, rounded photo crops |
| 05 | Ink Tide | watercolor pigment on paper | touch the ink drop | wet pigment blooms → paper dissolves through blur → color settles | irregular masks, fluid edges, ink-wash story section |
| 06 | Message to Shore | bottle floating in the tide | lift the cork | cork rises → water surface drains downward → foam drifts away | sea-glass cards, tide-colored sections, shell guestbook |
| 07 | One Take | Super 8 frame and projector beam | flip the projector switch | switch clicks → reels spin → one vertical film gate unspools with a flash | film perforations, sepia stills, dotted reel separators |
| 08 | Giấy Dó Shadow Theatre | Vietnamese paper screen and lantern | pull the red tassel | lantern blooms → giấy dó becomes translucent → shadow bloom fades | hỷ-red family panel, lotus shadows, double-rule dividers |
| 09 | Astrolabe Promise | star map and brass rings | touch the meeting star | star ignites → concentric rings contract → central iris reveals the names | night-sky grid, circular hero, constellation guestbook |
| 10 | Seoul Rain Window | wet glass, city neon | sweep the wiper tab | diagonal wiper clears fog → rain film slides → neon reflections bloom | cool glass cards, diagonal rain cue, night story panel |

The opening uses a fixed 3.1s handoff after activation (2.52s fade delay + reveal transition). With reduced motion enabled, the stage resolves immediately while preserving the same content hierarchy.
